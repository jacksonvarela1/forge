DAY 1 – Chest + Biceps + Forearms

Primer: Plyo Push-Up – 3 × 5 + Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Incline Dumbbell Press – 2 × 6–10 (bail bells to the sides)
2. Machine Chest Press – 2 × 8–12
3. Cable Fly – 2 × 10–15 (stop at torso line)
4. Cable Lateral Raise – 3 × 12–20
5. Incline Dumbbell Curl – 2 × 8–12 (slow into the stretch)
6. Rope Hammer Curl – 2 × 10–15

Forearms
7. Towel Dead Hang – 2 × 30–45 sec
8. Seated Wrist Extension – 2 × 15–20

Stretch: Doorway Pec – 45 sec per side · Thoracic Extension over Bench – 45 sec · Wrist Flexor + Extensor – 30 sec each

 DAY 2 – Back + Triceps + Abs

Primer: Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Neutral-Grip Pulldown – 2 × 8–12
2. Chest-Supported T-Bar Row – 2 × 8–12 (straps on)
3. Single-Arm Cable Row – 2 × 10–12 per side
4. Reverse Pec Deck – 2 × 12–20 superset Behind-the-Back Cable Lateral Raise – 2 × 12–20
5. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension – 2 × 10–15
6. Rope Pushdown – 2 × 10–15

Abs
7. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15
8. Hanging Knee Hugs – 2 × 10–15

Stretch: Overhead Lat + Triceps Stretch – 30 sec per side · Thread-the-Needle – 8 per side · Active Hang – 30 sec

⸻

 DAY 3 – Hamstring-Focused Lower + Abs

Primer: Pogo Hops – 2 × 10

1. Nordic Hamstring Curl – 1 × 4–6 (hands catch)
2. 45 Degree Back Extension – 2 × 10–15 (plate at chest, no hyperextension)
3. Seated Leg Curl – 2 × 10–15
4. Leg Press – 2 × 8–12 (deep, safeties set)
5. Seated Calf Raise – 3 × 10–15 (2-sec pause at stretch)
• Tibialis Raise – 2 × 12–20
6. Copenhagen Adductor Hold – 1 × 30–45 sec per side (stop at first hip drop) superset Banded Lateral Step-Out – 1 × 12–15 per side

Abs
7. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15
8. Hanging Knee Hugs – 2 × 10–15

Stretch: Standing Hamstring – 45 sec per side · Adductor Rock-Back – 10 per side · 90/90 Hip Switch – 45 sec per side

 DAY 4 – Rest

DAY 5 – Upper + Forearms

Primer: Plyo Push-Up – 3 × 5 + Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Weighted Dips – 2 × 6–10 (box behind feet, 1-sec touch)
2. Neutral-Grip Pull-Up – 2 × 6–10 (20-sec dead hang after last set)
3. Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row – 2 × 10–12
4. Cable Lateral Raise – 3 × 12–20 superset Reverse Pec Deck – 2 × 12–20
5. Face-Away Cable Curl – 2 × 10–15
6. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension – 2 × 10–15

Forearms
7. Dumbbell Reverse Curl – 2 × 10–12
8. Heavy Farmer's Hold – 2 × 30–40 sec per side (set down at first slip)

Stretch: Doorway Pec – 45 sec per side · Thread-the-Needle – 8 per side · Wrist Flexor + Extensor – 30 sec each

 DAY 6 – Quad-Focused Lower + Abs

Primer: Pogo Hops – 2 × 10 barefoot + Box Jump – 3 × 3 + Rim Attempts – 4 max (skip if sore, stop when sluggish)

1. Back Squat – 2 × 6–10 (pins set, 2-sec pause first set)
2. Bulgarian Split Squat – 2 × 8–12 per side (stop at wobble, rack within reach)
3. Leg Extension – 2 × 10–15
4. Seated Leg Curl – 2 × 10–15
5. Single-Leg Standing Calf Raise – 3 × 10–15 per side (2-sec pause at stretch)
• Tibialis Raise – 2 × 12–20
6. Copenhagen Adductor Hold – 1 × 30–45 sec per side superset Banded Lateral Step-Out – 1 × 12–15 per side

Abs
7. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15
8. Hanging Knee Hugs – 2 × 10–15

Stretch: Couch Stretch – 45 sec per side · Cossack Squat – 8 per side · Wall Ankle Dorsiflexion – 10 per side

 DAY 7 – Rest
