DAY 1 – Chest + Biceps + Forearms

Primer: Plyo Push-Up – 3 × 5 + Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Incline Dumbbell Press (15–30°) – 3 × 6–10 (bells outside shoulders, 3 s down, hold the bottom 1–2 s on set 1 only; 1–2 RIR; rest 2–3 min)
2. Machine Chest Press – 2 × 8–12 (handles mid-chest, upper arms level with torso at the stretch, hold 1–2 s, no shrug; 1–2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
3. Cable Fly – 2 × 10–15 (low cables, slight elbow bend, stop at torso line, moderate load, 2 s hold, no bounce; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
4. Cable Lateral Raise – 3 × 12–20 per side (cross-body start, 1 s hold stretched, above parallel; last set plus 5 half-reps; 0–1 RIR; rest 45 sec)
5. Incline Dumbbell Curl (45–60° bench) – 3 × 8–12 (arms hang straight, hold 1–2 s stretched, no swing, shoulders on the bench; 2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
6. Face-Away Cable Curl (Bayesian curl) – 2 × 10–15 per side (arm slightly behind torso, hold stretch 1–2 s, no swing; 1–2 RIR; rest 45 sec)

Forearms
7. Seated Wrist Curl – 2 × 15–25 (forearms on thighs, bar to fingertips, hold 1–2 s at full stretch, wrist only, light; 2 RIR; rest 45 sec)
8. Seated Wrist Extension – 2 × 15–25 (palms down, lower to full flexion, hold 1–2 s at full stretch, wrist only, light; 2 RIR; rest 45 sec)

Stretch: Doorway Pec – 45 sec per side · Thoracic Extension over Bench – 45 sec · Wrist Flexor + Extensor – 30 sec each

Weeks 1–2: Plyo push-up 2 × 5. 2 sets on incline press, lateral and incline curl; bench at 60°. 4 RIR in W1, 3 RIR in W2. No half-reps.

 DAY 2 – Back + Triceps + Abs

Primer: Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown – 2 × 15

1. Neutral-Grip Pulldown – 3 × 8–12 (full reach, shoulders down, hold 1–2 s on set 1, elbows to ribs; straps allowed; 1–2 RIR; rest 2–3 min)
2. Chest-Supported T-Bar Row – 2 × 8–12 (chest on pad, wide elbows, protract and hold 1–2 s on set 1; straps allowed; 1–2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
3. Single-Arm Cable Row – 2 × 10–12 per side (seated or half-kneeling, torso still, tucked elbow, hold 1–2 s at stretch; straps allowed; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
4. Reverse Pec Deck – 2 × 12–20 (chest on pad, reach to full stretch, hold 1–2 s, sweep wide, no shrug; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec) superset Behind-the-Back Cable Lateral Raise – 2 × 12–20 per side (cable crosses just behind the hip, hold 1–2 s stretched, lift above parallel; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
5. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension – 3 × 10–15 (elbows high and forward, deep stretch hold 1–2 s, no flaring; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
6. EZ-Bar Skullcrusher – 2 × 10–12 (bar to just behind head, hold stretch 1–2 s, elbows fixed; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)

Abs
7. Half-Kneeling Pallof Press – 2 × 8–10 per side (press out, hold 3 s, ribs down, hips square, no twist; 2 RIR; rest 45 sec)
8. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15 (start spine extended, curl ribs to pelvis, hold 1–2 s squeezed, hips fixed; 1–2 RIR; rest 45 sec)

Stretch: Overhead Lat + Triceps Stretch – 45 sec per side · Thread-the-Needle – 8 per side · Child's Pose Lat Reach – 45 sec

Weeks 1–2: 2 sets on pulldown and overhead extension. 4 RIR in W1, 3 RIR in W2. Rope crunch light.

 DAY 3 – Hamstring-Focused Lower + Forearms

Primer: Pogo Hops – 2 × 10 + Banded Lateral Step-Out – 1 × 12–15 per side

1. Romanian Deadlift – 3 × 6–8 (lower until hips stop moving back, spine flat, hold 1–2 s on set 1; straps mandatory; 2 RIR; rest 2–3 min)
2. Nordic Hamstring Curl – 2 × 4–6 (hips straight, 4–5 s down, catch with hands; 12 reps a week at most; no failure; rest 2–3 min)
3. Seated Leg Curl – 2 × 10–15 (lean forward over the thighs, hips pinned, hold 1–2 s stretched, 3 s down; 0–1 RIR; rest 90 sec)
4. Deep Leg Press – 2 × 8–12 (feet low, deepest controlled rep before pelvis tucks, hold 1–2 s on set 1; 1–2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
5. Standing Calf Raise – 3 × 10–15 (full heel drop, hold stretch 2–3 s, last set plus 5 half-reps; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec)
• Tibialis Raise – 2 × 12–20 (back on wall, toes up fully, hold 1–2 s at top; 0–1 RIR; rest 45 sec)
6. Copenhagen Adductor Hold – 1 × 30–45 sec per side (knee on bench first, foot on bench later; hips stacked, stop before pain; stop 5 s short; rest 60 sec)

Forearms
7. EZ-Bar Reverse Curl – 2 × 10–15 (overhand grip, elbows pinned, hold 1 s at stretch, 3 s down; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
8. Suitcase Hold – 2 × 30–40 sec per side (one dumbbell, about 70% of your 40 s max, upright, no lean or shrug; stop 5 s short; rest 60 sec)

Stretch: Standing Hamstring – 45 sec per side, easy, no bounce · Adductor Rock-Back – 45 sec · 90/90 Hip Switch – 8 per side

Weeks 1–2: 2 sets on RDL and calves. RDL stops mid-range. Nordic: eccentric to a box, 2 × 3. Copenhagen: knee on bench, 20–25 s. Leg press hold 1 s.

DAY 5 – Upper + Forearms

Primer: Plyo Push-Up – 3 × 5 + Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Weighted Dip – 3 × 8–12 (chest lean, upper arms parallel, touch 1 s, no weighted hold, add load after 12 clean; 2 RIR; rest 2–3 min)
2. Neutral-Grip Pull-Up – 2 × 6–10 (active hang 1 s, chest to bar, 3 s down; 1–2 RIR; rest 2–3 min)
3. Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row – 2 × 10–12 (chest on 30–45° bench, wide elbows, protract and hold 1–2 s on set 1; straps allowed; 1–2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
4. Cable Lateral Raise – 3 × 12–20 per side (cross-body start, 1 s hold stretched, above parallel; set 3 solo plus 5 half-reps; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec) superset Reverse Pec Deck – 2 × 12–20 (chest on pad, reach to full stretch, hold 1–2 s, sweep wide, no shrug; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec)
5. Preacher Curl – 3 × 8–12 (arm extended, hold 1–2 s, no bounce out of the bottom, stop short of lockout; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec)
6. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension – 2 × 10–15 (elbows high and forward, deep stretch hold 1–2 s, no flaring; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec)

Forearms
7. Dumbbell Reverse Curl – 2 × 10–15 (overhand grip, elbows pinned, hold 1 s at stretch, 3 s down; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
8. Active Towel Hang – 2 × 20–30 sec (towel over the bar, shoulders packed, no swing; stop 5 s short; rest 60 sec)

Stretch: Doorway Pec – 45 sec per side · Thread-the-Needle – 8 per side · Wrist Flexor + Extensor – 30 sec each

Weeks 1–2: Plyo push-up 2 × 5. 2 sets on dip, lateral and preacher. Towel hang 20 s. 4 RIR in W1, 3 RIR in W2. No half-reps.

 DAY 6 – Quad-Focused Lower + Abs

Primer: Pogo Hops – 2 × 10 + Box Jump – 3 × 3 + Rim Attempts – 4–6 attempts

1. Back Squat – 3 × 6–10 (stop at pelvic tuck, safeties just below; set 1 hold 2–3 s, then touch; 2 RIR; rest 2–3 min)
2. Bulgarian Split Squat – 2 × 8–12 per side (front knee over toes, heel down, deepest controlled rep, hold 1–2 s on set 1; 1–2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
3. Reclined Leg Extension – 2 × 10–15 (seat reclined, hold 1–2 s deep, 3 s down; last set plus 5 half-reps; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec)
4. Seated Leg Curl – 2 × 10–15 (gentle: lean forward, hips pinned, hold 1–2 s stretched; 2 RIR; rest 90 sec)
5. Single-Leg Calf Raise – 3 × 10–15 per side (step edge, dumbbell in hand, full heel drop, hold 2–3 s, last set plus half-reps; 0–1 RIR; rest 60 sec)
• Tibialis Raise – 2 × 12–20 (back on wall, toes up fully, hold 1–2 s at top; 0–1 RIR; rest 45 sec)
6. Copenhagen Adductor Hold – 1 × 30–45 sec per side (knee on bench first, foot on bench later; hips stacked, stop before pain; stop 5 s short; rest 60 sec)

Abs
7. Ab Wheel Rollout – 2 × 6–10 (from knees, ribs down, roll to longest flat-back reach, hold 1–2 s; 1–2 RIR; rest 60 sec)
8. Half-Kneeling Landmine Rotation – 2 × 8–10 per side (rotate from ribcage, hips square, 3 s return, light load; 2 RIR; rest 45 sec)

Stretch: Couch Stretch – 45 sec per side · Wall Ankle Dorsiflexion – 10 per side · Deep Squat Prying Hold – 45 sec

Weeks 1–2: Jumps need normal hamstrings: box jump 2 × 3, 4 rim attempts. 2-set squat and calves. Split squat bodyweight, mid-range. Curl 4 RIR, 70–80% Day 3 load. Copenhagen 20–25 s.

NOTES

Effort: RIR is reps left in reserve. The effort on each line is the full-dose value (week 5 on); weeks 1–4 follow the ramp. Dips, squat, RDL and Nordics never go below 2 RIR. "No failure" means stop a rep or two before form breaks; "stop 5 s short" means end a hold 5 s before form or grip fails.
Depth and hold: deepest pain-free controlled position. Hold 1–2 s at the stretched end of every cable, machine and isolation rep (never longer than 3 s). On compounds hold on set 1, then touch at the same depth. Jumps are never paused. Half-reps (5, from the stretch) only on the last set of laterals, leg extension and calves.
Pain rule: a joint (not muscle burn) that pinches in the stretch means shorten the range, lighten, or swap the exercise.
Ramp: W1 4 RIR, 3-set items run 2 sets, new deep or held items at 60–70% of your guessed load. W2 3 RIR. W3 2–3 RIR, third set returns on incline press, pulldown and dip. W4 2 RIR, third set returns on the other 3-set items. W5 full dose. W6 deload: same loads, one set fewer on every 2–3 set exercise, 4–5 RIR, Nordic 1 × 4, Copenhagen back to knee on the bench (20–25 s), pogo 1 × 10 on Days 3 and 6, plyo push-ups 1 × 5 and band pull-aparts 1 × 20 on Days 1 and 5, Day 6 box jump 2 × 3 with no rim attempts. After W6 repeat W2–W5 with heavier loads, and deload every 5th or 6th week.
Nordic: W1–2 2 × 3 eccentric to a box, W3–4 2 × 4 band-assisted, W5+ 2 × 4–6, then keep that dose steady (12 reps a week at most).
Copenhagen: W1–2 knee on the bench, 20–25 s; W3–4 knee on the bench, 25–30 s; W5 foot on the bench, 20–30 s building to 30–45 s. Step up only if groin soreness 24 h later is 4/10 or less.
Day 6 curl: W1–2 4 RIR, W3–4 3 RIR, W5+ 2 RIR, at about 70–80% of Day 3 curl weight in W1–4, never 0–1 RIR. It lands about 72 h after the Nordics. If your hamstrings are above 3/10 sore on Day 6, skip the box jumps and rim attempts and keep the pogo hops. In W1–2 they run only if your hamstrings feel normal.
Progression: when every set tops the rep range at the written effort in two sessions running, add the smallest load and restart at the bottom. Timed holds add 5 s a session.
Warm-up: 3–5 min easy cardio, then 2 ramp sets before the first compound (about 50% × 5, 75% × 3) and 1 before the next. In W1 pick loads that leave 4 reps in reserve and log load × reps every set. Missed day: skip it, never double up; miss two or more and repeat that ramp week.
