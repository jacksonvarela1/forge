DAY 1 – Chest + Biceps + Forearms

Primer: Plyo Push-Up – 3 × 5 + Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Incline Dumbbell Press – 2 × 6–10 (bail bells to the sides)
2. Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press – 2 × 8–12
3. Dumbbell Fly – 3 × 10–15 (soft elbows, stop at the stretch)
4. Standing Dumbbell Lateral Raise – 3 × 12–20
5. Incline Dumbbell Curl – 2 × 8–12 (slow into the stretch)
6. Rope Hammer Curl – 2 × 10–15

Forearms
7. Cable Forearm Curl – 2 × 15–20 (elbows tucked, wrists only)
8. Seated Wrist Extension – 2 × 15–20

Stretch: Doorway Pec – 45 sec per side · Thoracic Extension over Bench – 45 sec · Wrist Flexor + Extensor – 30 sec each

 DAY 2 – Back + Triceps + Abs

Primer: Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Neutral-Grip Pulldown – 2 × 8–12
2. Chest-Supported T-Bar Row – 2 × 8–12 (straps on)
3. Archer Pullback – 2 × 12–15 per side (elbow out and back, bow arm straight)
4. Dumbbell Shrug – 2 × 10–15 (straps on, 1-sec pause at top)
5. Overhead Rope Triceps Extension – 2 × 10–15
6. Rope Pushdown – 2 × 10–15

Abs
7. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15
8. Hanging Knee Hugs – 2 × 10–15

Stretch: Overhead Lat + Triceps – 30 sec per side · Thread-the-Needle – 8 per side · Child's Pose Lat Reach – 45 sec

⸻

 DAY 3 – Hamstring-Focused Lower + Abs

Primer: Pogo Hops – 2 × 10 + Broad Jumps – 3 × 3

1. Stiff-Leg Deadlift – 2 × 6–10 (straps on, back flat, last clean rep)
2. Seated Leg Curl – 2 × 10–15
3. 45 Degree Back Extension – 2 × 10–15 (plate at chest, no hyperextension)
4. Leg Extension – 2 × 10–15
5. Standing Calf Raise – 2 × 10–15 (2-sec pause at stretch)
• Tibialis Raise – 2 × 12–20
6. Hip Adductor Machine – 2 × 12–15 superset Hip Abductor Machine – 2 × 12–15

Abs
7. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15
8. Hanging Knee Hugs – 2 × 10–15

Stretch: Standing Hamstring – 45 sec per side · Adductor Rock-Back – 10 per side · 90/90 Hip Switch – 45 sec per side

DAY 5 – Upper + Forearms

Primer: Plyo Push-Up – 3 × 5 + Band Pull-Apart – 2 × 20

1. Weighted Dips – 2 × 6–10 (box behind feet, 1-sec touch at parallel)
2. Wide-Grip Pull-Up – 2 × 6–10
3. One-Arm Dumbbell Row – 2 × 8–12 per side (hand on bench, no twist)
4. Seated Dumbbell Lateral Raise – 3 × 12–20
5. Preacher Curl – 2 × 8–12 (slow into the stretch, stop short of lockout)
6. EZ-Bar Skull Crusher – 2 × 10–12 (elbows fixed, bar behind head, bail backward)

Forearms
7. Dumbbell Reverse Curl – 2 × 10–12
8. Cable Forearm Curl – 2 × 15–20

Stretch: Doorway Pec – 45 sec per side · Thread-the-Needle – 8 per side · Wrist Flexor + Extensor – 30 sec each

 DAY 6 – Quad-Focused Lower + Abs

Primer: Pogo Hops – 2 × 10 barefoot + Box Jump – 3 × 3 + Rim Attempts – 4 max (skip if sore, stop when sluggish)

1. Seated Leg Curl – 1 × 8–12
2. Back Squat – 2 × 6–10 (pins just below your depth, 2-sec pause first set)
3. Bulgarian Split Squat – 2 × 8–12 per side (stop at wobble, rack within reach)
4. Leg Extension – 2 × 10–15
5. Single-Leg Standing Calf Raise – 2 × 10–15 per side (2-sec pause at stretch)
• Tibialis Raise – 2 × 12–20
6. Hip Adductor Machine – 2 × 12–15 superset Hip Abductor Machine – 2 × 12–15

Abs
7. Weighted Cable Rope Crunch – 2 × 10–15
8. Hanging Knee Hugs – 2 × 10–15

Stretch: Couch Stretch – 45 sec per side · Cossack Squat – 8 per side · Wall Ankle Dorsiflexion – 10 per side
