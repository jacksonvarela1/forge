# Five-day split: Chest/Bi, Back/Tri, Hamstrings, rest, Upper, Quads, rest

> Growth work is the six numbered slots: deep reps with a held stretch at moderate loads, built from cable, machine and chest-supported picks in the Nippard style, with set counts and RIR in the Renaissance Periodization (RP) style. Add-ons are the primers, Nordic, Copenhagen, tibialis, the forearm or abs block and the stretch line, a light ankle, hamstring and adductor layer. Each day is shaped by what sits before and after it. It gives up overhead pressing, high-end calf, rear-delt and abs volume, and heavy grip work the day before D6.

Every training day has the same shape: a short primer, **6 main exercises**, then **2 for forearms or abs** (always exactly one of the two), then a stretch line. Sets are 1 to 3 per exercise. Times: the mid-pace estimate rounded to 5 min (50 s per working set, 45 s between exercises, rests as written), including 2 uncounted ramp sets before the first compound, one feeler set, the primer and the stretch line; D6 includes the 15-18 minute jump primer; the 3-5 min general warm-up is extra; weeks 1-2 run about 3-4 min shorter and the deload about 12 min shorter.

## Rules (read once)

- **Rest.** Use the Rest column; compounds get the longest rests, isolation and holds the shortest.
- **Effort.** RIR is reps in reserve. W1-4 follow the ramp RIR on every set (the D6 curl has its own ladder); from W5 each row's own RIR applies, and on 0-1 RIR rows only the last set goes that far. Dips, squat, RDL and Nordics never go below 2 RIR. "No failure" means stop a rep or two before form breaks; "stop 5 s short" means end a hold 5 s before form or grip fails.
- **Depth and hold.** Hold 1-2 s at the stretched end of every rep (the cue overrides; nothing longer than 3 s). Compounds hold on set 1, then touch at the same depth with no hold, because holding every set costs load (my design choice, see Sources); dips are a 1 s touch. Jumps are never paused. Half-reps: 5 partials from the stretch after the last full rep, on the last set of laterals, leg extension and calves only.
- **Pain rule.** Train to the deepest pain-free controlled position. A joint (not muscle burn) that pinches in the stretch means shorten the range, lighten, or use the swap. This applies to knee pain on squats, elbow ache on curls and extensions, and low-back rounding.
- **Progression.** Depth and hold stay fixed. When every set tops the rep range at the row RIR in two sessions running, add the smallest load and restart low. Timed holds: +5 s a session within ramp caps.
- **Deload.** W6: same loads, one set fewer on every 2-3 set exercise (min 1), 4-5 RIR. D6 jumps: pogo 1 × 10, box jump 2 × 3, no rim attempts. D3 pogo 1 × 10, plyo push-ups 1 × 5, pull-aparts 1 × 20. Nordic 1 × 4. Copenhagen back to knee on bench, 20-25 s.
- **Warm-up, loads, logging.** Warm-up: 3-5 min easy cardio, then 2 ramp sets before the first compound (about 50% × 5, 75% × 3) and 1 before the next; none count. W1: pick loads leaving 4 reps in reserve; log load × reps every set. Missed day: skip it, never double up; miss 2 or more, repeat that ramp week.

**Ramp (first 6 weeks)**

- W1: 4 RIR on every set; 3-set items run 2 sets; items new to you at 60-70% of your guessed load; no half-reps; squat hold 1 s. Each day's card lists its W1-2 caps.
- W2: 3 RIR; same caps and the same 2-set start.
- W3: 2-3 RIR; full depth and holds return; plyo push-up 3 × 5 and box jump 3 × 3 return; third set returns on incline press, pulldown and dip if recovered; half-reps only on sets that ended at 1-2 RIR.
- W4: 2 RIR; third set returns on every other 3-set item if recovered.
- W5: full dose from here: each row's own RIR, Nordic 2 × 4-6, Copenhagen foot on bench, rim attempts up to 6.
- W6: deload.
- Nordic ladder: W1-2 2 × 3 eccentric to a box, W3-4 2 × 4 band-assisted, W5+ 2 × 4-6, then keep that dose steady; weekly ceiling 12 reps.
- Copenhagen, both lower days: W1-2 knee on bench 20-25 s, W3-4 knee on bench 25-30 s, W5 foot on bench 20-30 s toward 30-45 s; step up only if groin soreness 24 h later is 4/10 or less.
- D6 curl runs its own ladder: W1-2 4 RIR, W3-4 3 RIR, W5+ 2 RIR, starting at about 70-80% of D3's curl weight in W1-4 and stopping at the RIR or 15 reps, never 0-1 RIR.
- D6 jumps: if hamstrings are above 3/10 sore, skip box jumps and rim attempts and keep pogo only; in W1-2 they run only if hamstrings feel normal; rim attempts stay at 4 until W5.
- Light through W3: D2 rope crunch, D5 towel hang at 20 s.
- After W6: repeat W2 to W5 with heavier loads, deload every 5th or 6th week, swap an exercise only at a deload.

## Week notes

- D1 to D2 (next day): biceps get 5 direct sets on D1 and about 3.5 fractional on D2; triceps get no direct work on D1 (no dips, no close-grip pressing) and about 2.5 fractional from presses, so they arrive moderately fresh. D1 curls never go below 1 RIR and wrist work stops at 2 RIR.
- D2 to D3 (next day): no hangs, no grip block and no free-standing rows on D2; straps are allowed on the pulldown and rows, mandatory on the RDL; every pull stops at 1-2 RIR. If your low back is tight before D3, replace the RDL with seated leg curl 3 × 10-12 and keep the Nordic at its box version; from W3 the minimum RDL is 2 sets at 3 RIR, never to full depth.
- Nordic window: soreness after Nordics is reported on days 2 to 3 (a small study at a higher dose than this plan), which is D5 and D6. D5 has no hinge, leg curl or leg jumps; the D6 curl is gentle and the D6 jump gate applies.
- D3 to D4: D4 is a rest day right after the heaviest hamstring session. If you add hard conditioning or a hard sport session on D4, keep it low on ground impact. Mitigation: the Nordic dose is capped on a fixed ladder, the RDL stops at 2 RIR, nothing above pogo height on D3.
- Grip plan: D1 wrist-only, no grip before the RDL, the one heavy hold (suitcase) lives on D3 behind a rest day, D5 grip is lightly worked, D6 abs are low-grip, the split squat uses a rack for balance (bodyweight or a light dumbbell). If hands felt flat last D6, drop the D5 towel hang; if the pull-ups already felt grip-limited, drop it that day.
- Low-recovery cut order: D2 abs block, then one D1 biceps set, then the D2 skullcrusher, then the D2 rear-delt and lateral pair, then the D5 towel hang; never the pulldown, one row or the overhead extension. If first-set reps fall more than 2 on the pulldown or overhead extension, move 1 set to D5 for two weeks. D2 has 16 main sets including the superset partner (20 with the block).
- Elbow rule: if either elbow joint (not the muscle) aches two sessions running, swap the stretched curl for an incline curl at 60 degrees, and the overhead extension or skullcrusher for a rope pushdown, for two weeks.
- D5 to D6: D5 rows are chest-supported and there is no abs block, so trunk and grip are fresh for D6 squats. Only D6 carries max-intent leg jumps (about 35 contacts), 72 h after D3; D1 and D5 plyo push-ups are four days apart one way and three the other. If the front of the shoulder is sore from dips, use a wider bar grip or the hack squat swap on D6.

## Gear and fallbacks

- T-bar row: no T-bar means the chest-supported row machine, else the D5 dumbbell row on a 30-45° bench.
- Reverse pec deck: a rope face pull at head height, or a chest-down rear-delt raise on an incline bench.
- Nordic: needs a heel anchor (partner, Nordic bench or a loaded barbell under a rack); W1-2 also a box, W3-4 a band anchored overhead.
- Copenhagen: any bench or sturdy box at knee height; start with the knee on it, move to the foot later.
- Tibialis raise: the wall version is bodyweight; progress by reps, then a slower lower.
- Landmine rotation: a barbell wedged in a corner with a towel round the end; or a half-kneeling cable rotation.
- EZ bar (skullcrusher, reverse curl): a straight bar or dumbbells work.
- Suitcase hold: the heaviest dumbbell or kettlebell you have.

## What changed from your old split

- New order: chest + biceps, back + triceps, hamstring lower, rest, upper, quad lower, rest.
- Direct weekly sets in your old split, counted the same way, before to after: chest 5 to 10, back 8 to 11, side delts 5 to 8, rear delts 2 to 4, biceps 2 to 8 (5 on D1, 3 on D5), triceps 5 to 7, forearms 8 to 12, calves 4 to 6, front delts 2 to 0, rectus-abs sets 6 to 4, hamstrings 6 to 7 without the Nordic, quads 9 to 9. Glutes fell from 10 to 0 only because squat-pattern lifts and the RDL now credit quads and hamstrings alone.
- Moved: weighted dips and pull-ups to D5 only; T-bar row now chest-supported on D2; Pallof and rope crunch from D3 to D2; landmine rotation into the D6 abs block; heavy hold from D5 (farmer) to D3 (suitcase); banded step-out into the D3 primer; D6 leg curl from 1 set in slot 1 to 2 sets in slot 4.
- Cut: DB shoulder press (front delts 0 direct); hanging straight-leg raise (rectus-abs sets fell from 6 to 4, replaced by the anti-rotation work you named as keepers); broad jumps (off D3); trap-bar jump option; second banded step-out; face pull (replaced by reverse pec deck); pull-up dead hang; single-arm cable pulldown (pulldown is now bilateral, the single-arm row stays); towel hang dose cut to 2 × 20-30 s.
- Seated calf raise became a second standing day: the straight-knee stretch is what grows the gastrocnemius, and the soleus grew about the same either way in the sourced calf trial; swap seated back in if you miss it.
- Rep ranges moved up so deep, held reps stay at moderate load: squat 5-8 to 6-10, dips 6-10 to 8-12, Bulgarian split squat 6-8 to 8-12 per side (it keeps its name), leg curl 8-12 to 10-15, calves to 10-15. RDL stays 3 × 6-8. Stretch lines are now day-specific: couch stretch on D6 only, 90/90 on D3 only, new lines on D2.
- Kept: back squat with the set 1 hold, RDL 3 sets, Nordic, Copenhagen, tibialis add-ons on both lower days, pogo and box jump with rim attempts, plyo push-ups, the primer, numbered-slot and stretch-line format.

## Trade-offs

- No overhead pressing: the DB shoulder press is cut, so front delts get 0 direct sets and rely on chest work for about 5 fractional sets.
- Calves are 6 direct sets (standing 3 on D3, single-leg 3 on D6) against the 8-10 target, the bottom of the Renaissance Periodization (RP) 6-16 range; tibialis is 4 sets a week.
- Several muscles sit at the floor of the format: side delts 8, rear delts 4, biceps 8, and abs 4 direct, plus 4 anti-rotation oblique sets; core total 8 is the format's ceiling.
- Forearms read 12 sets: 8 dynamic plus 4 grip-hold sets (suitcase hold, towel hang).
- No direct neck, glute, erector or cuff slot; band pull-aparts and the stretch lines carry shoulder health, and the format has no room for more. Also out: reverse Nordic and sissy squat; Copenhagen stays at slot 6; D6 stays the heaviest leg session.
- The Nordic is an athletic drill and is not counted: hamstrings are 7 direct sets (RDL 3 and curl 2 on D3, curl 2 on D6), or 9 with the Nordic, so D3 carries 7 hard hamstring sets; there is no direct glute or erector slot.
